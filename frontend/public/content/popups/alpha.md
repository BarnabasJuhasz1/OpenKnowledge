# OpenKnowledge is in Alpha

You are using an early **Alpha** release. The core workflow — search, OK-score,
and the citation graphs — works today, but expect rough edges while we build
toward a stable release.

## What that means

- **Things will change.** Features, scoring, and the interface may shift between
  updates without notice.
- **Data is partial.** This alpha searches the Semantic Scholar corpus, and not
  every paper has full metadata or an abstract yet. More sources are planned.
- **You may hit bugs.** If something looks wrong, it might be us, not you.

## Help us improve

Found a bug or have an idea? Use the feedback button in the app, or open an
issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/BarnabasJuhasz1/OpenKnowledge). Thanks for
trying OpenKnowledge early — your feedback genuinely shapes what we build next.
